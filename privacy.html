This page describes how the current Gateway Global Trade website inquiry form works.

Inquiry form

The inquiry form runs in your browser. It does not send the information you enter to Gateway Global Trade or store it on this website. When you prepare an inquiry, the site attempts to copy the text to your clipboard so you can share it with your Gateway Global Trade contact. You can also select and copy the text shown on screen.

Website fonts

This site requests typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those font files. You can use the site with your browser's fallback fonts if that connection is unavailable.

Questions

No direct privacy contact has been provided for this website yet. The Contact page explains the current inquiry process.
